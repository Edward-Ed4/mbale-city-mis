Why sanitation inequality matters

Consequences highlighted in the research presentation

Public health

Increased exposure to waterborne diseases and higher household healthcare expenditure.

Environment

Pollution and contamination can affect neighbourhoods and water sources.

Privacy, dignity & safety

Low sanitation access can disproportionately affect women and girls.

Climate resilience

Flood-prone communities can face greater sanitation risks during climate-related events.
