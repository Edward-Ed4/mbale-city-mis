Using the evidence for allocation

Suggested sequence for a validated planning process

1. Validate

Confirm household definitions, ward boundaries, source period, and sanitation indicators with UBOS and Council data teams.

2. Compare

Compare ward-level gaps alongside vulnerability, flood exposure, and intervention coverage—not the synthetic values in this demo.

3. Monitor

Agree on measurable follow-up indicators, responsible agencies, update frequency, and documented data quality checks.
