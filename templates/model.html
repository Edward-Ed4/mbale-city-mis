How to explain the result during a presentation

Household size

The reported coefficient is negative (−0.6479), and p = 0.003. In the fitted model, larger household size is associated with a lower modeled sanitation-access proportion; the association is statistically significant at the 5% level.

Unemployment

The reported coefficient is negative (−0.0235), and p = 0.017. Unemployment is associated with lower modeled sanitation access in this model; this result is statistically significant at the 5% level.

Statistical caution

These are associations from the reported model, not proof of cause and effect. The coefficient scale, covariate definitions, diagnostics, and original output should be checked before interpreting effect size.

A p-value of 0.000 for the constant is displayed as reported; it typically represents rounding, not a probability that is literally zero. The slides report 58 ward observations; this is not evidence that the illustrative register values below reproduce the fitted model input.
